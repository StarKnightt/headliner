# Earth texture

`earth-night-4k.jpg` (4096×2048) and `earth-night-2k.jpg` (2048×1024) are downscaled from
NASA Earth Observatory's **Black Marble 2016** global night-lights composite
(Suomi NPP VIIRS). NASA imagery is in the public domain.

Source: NASA Earth Observatory, "Earth at Night" — https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/NightLights
Credit: NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Suomi NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Román, NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center.
